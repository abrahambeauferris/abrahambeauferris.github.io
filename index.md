## Projects

### Deep Albedo: A Spatially Aware Autoencoder Approach to Interactive Human Skin Rendering  
_Technologies: C++, Python, OpenCV, UE5_

![Deep Albedo Project](assets/images/deep-albedo.png)

Deep Albedo represents a significant stride in combining physics-based rendering with neural network methodologies. The inspiration for this project came from the intricate challenge of simulating human skin with all its unique subtleties. I embarked on developing an autoencoder framework that would harness the power of Monte Carlo photon simulations to capture the complex interactions between light and skin surfaces. In a setting that demanded both scientific rigor and creative experimentation, I collaborated with experts from the University of British Columbia and Huawei Technologies Canada. The system we developed dynamically adjusts simulated skin tones based on varying lighting and environmental conditions, a breakthrough that culminated in a showcase at SIGGRAPH Asia 2023. This project not only pushed the envelope in interactive rendering but also laid the groundwork for further advancements in real-time biophysical simulations.

### Enhancing the Graphical Fidelity of the SurgiSim Platform  
_Technologies: Unreal Engine 4, C++_

![Honours Project](assets/images/surgisim-platform-2.png)

For my honours research, I was presented with the formidable task of elevating the visual and interactive quality of the SurgiSim VR surgical training platform. The project revolved around creating an environment that was not only photorealistic but also responsive and immersive—a critical requirement for effective surgical training. To accomplish this, I explored and integrated advanced rendering techniques, including directional ambient occlusion and subsurface scattering, which simulated the nuanced interplay of light and shadow in soft tissues. Additionally, I implemented dynamic resolution scaling to maintain smooth performance even during computationally intensive scenarios. This blend of visual refinement and performance optimization resulted in a significantly enhanced training tool, one that provides a more realistic and engaging experience for users. The improvements have had a measurable impact on both the system’s functionality and its educational value, underscoring the vital role of optimized graphics in modern simulation systems.
